# CareWell OTC — Studio Strategy

1. **Customer:** Families and caregivers who need OTC products without wading through pharmacy aisle confusion — they want dose clarity, age bands, and pickup speed, not diagnosis theater.
2. **5-second feel:** Calm clinical trust — mint-white light, readable type, green/teal action color (no alarm red), “someone organized this for me.”
3. **Signature:** Symptom Triage Wizard as the homepage hero — symptom tile → age band → matched SKUs with add-to-cart and PDP dose specs; educational disclaimer always visible.
4. **Type & color:** Atkinson Hyperlegible for display and body; palette `#F0FDF4` ground, `#15803D` primary, `#0369A1` accent for trust cues — high contrast, WCAG-minded.
5. **Journey:** Triage film hero → 3D dose-form preview → category panels → verified review list → shop filter/sort → deep PDP (gallery, variants, specs, FAQ) → WELL10 checkout → same-day pickup success.
6. **Motion:** Soft rise on hero copy; reduced-motion swaps video for poster and disables orbit spin; 44px touch targets on triage tiles.
7. **3D usefulness:** Scene3D shows bottle + capsule forms in the round — same SKUs as triage, not decorative chrome; static fallback when WebGL unavailable.
8. **Conversion hooks:** Offer banner (WELL10), floating AI with pharmacist-style canned answers, refill loyalty line, newsletter, sticky CTA to triage/shop, local store list in footer.
